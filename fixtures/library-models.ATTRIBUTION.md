# Third-party assets — library models

The headset's furniture library (`source:"primitive"` rows) uses 3D models from
**Poly Haven** (<https://polyhaven.com>).

## Licence

Every asset on Poly Haven is published as **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain
dedication): <https://polyhaven.com/license>. Poly Haven states there:

> You can use our assets for any purpose, including commercial work. You do not need
> to give credit or attribution when using them (although it is appreciated). You can
> redistribute them, share them around, include them when sharing your own work, or
> even in a product you sell.

Attribution is therefore not required. It is recorded here anyway, and per model in
`fixtures/library-models.json`.

No model in this project comes from a merchant, brand or manufacturer site, and no
login, paywall or API token was bypassed to obtain one.
